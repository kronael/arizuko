Company Brain

A daily and weekly digest of what the team built and decided.

The digest reads the team’s Discord threads and the GitHub repo for one window. Two research halves run in parallel: digest-work reports who built what, digest-discourse reports what was argued and decided. The digest skill combines them, ranks by consequence, has a blind reviewer check the draft, and edits one message in the team channel in place.

Every claim carries its source

Each line cites a PR number or a thread quote with its time, and says what changed since the last digest. The agent is never a speaker. A status such as agreed needs the last human message to agree.

Follow the setup guide to install the package and fill the group config.
