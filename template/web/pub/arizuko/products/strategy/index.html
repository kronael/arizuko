Domain researcher (Prometheus)

Tracks one domain continuously and delivers a structured briefing every week, plus deep dives on demand. Separates confirmed from inferred from speculative, every time.

Prometheus writes the same four sections every week

Prometheus watches the sources in facts/watchlist.md and writes the same four sections each week: an executive summary, the key developments, the signals to watch and the recommended actions. Every finding has a confidence marker and a citation in the text: the URL, the publication and the date. The briefings go into facts/reports/, so the archive is a directory that you can grep.

The useful part is the focus: what changed since the last briefing, not a new summary of the domain. A weekly report that reads like the last one is a report that nobody opens twice.

A sample exchange

user   anything on the licensing change?

agent  Confirmed: BSL 1.1 as of 2026-08-11 (vendor blog, 11 Aug).
       Inferred: the managed-service carve-out targets one competitor —
       wording matches their product, not the category.
       Speculative: a fork within a quarter. Two of the three top
       contributors have not signed the CLA.
       Implications: (1) your redistribution clause needs review.

The product ships four files

FileWhat it is
PRODUCT.mdthe manifest: name, brand, tagline and env hints
SOUL.mdwho the agent is; runed renames it to PERSONA.md at the first turn; analytical, marks confidence, cites in the text
facts/domain.mdthe domain, its main players and what matters in it
facts/watchlist.mdthe sources, companies and topics to track

The product also ships its tests in eval/.

A team that tracks one domain needs it

The product is for a team that must stay current in one domain and does it now with many browser tabs. It is for research, not for conversation: the output is a document, and the chat is where you ask for one.
