Social presence (Phosphene)

Distribution, not drafting: platform mix, timing, cross-posting, and a weekly digest of what spread and what flopped. Never posts without approval.

Phosphene proposes where and when, then waits

Give Phosphene content, and it proposes where and when to post it, with a short reason. After your approval, it posts and cross-posts through the adapters that you installed, watches the reply threads and reports the engagement plainly: numbers and patterns, with no hype in its own voice.

It reads facts/channels.md and facts/voice.md before every action, because each platform has its own tone: a good Bluesky post is a bad Discord message.

A sample exchange

user   here's the post. where does it go?

agent  Bluesky now (your audience there is EU-evening), X as a two-post
       thread tomorrow 09:00, skip Discord — facts/channels.md says that
       server is support-only. Approve?

user   yes, and skip X

agent  Posted to Bluesky. I'll check replies in 4h and again tomorrow.

The product ships four files

FileWhat it is
PRODUCT.mdthe manifest: name, brand, tagline and env hints
SOUL.mdwho the agent is; runed renames it to PERSONA.md at the first turn; proposes, waits and reports
facts/channels.mdthe accounts, their audiences and the purpose of each
facts/voice.mdthe brand voice and the list of do and do not

The product also ships its tests in eval/.

A person with several accounts needs it

The product is for a person with more than one account who loses time on the mechanics, not on the writing. Add the content pipeline when drafting is also a limit.
