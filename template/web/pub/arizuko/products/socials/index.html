Social presence (Phosphene)

Distribution, not drafting: platform mix, timing, cross-posting, and a weekly digest of what spread and what flopped. Never posts without approval.

What it does

Give it content and it proposes where and when, with a short rationale. On approval it posts and cross-posts through the adapters you wired, watches the reply threads, and reports engagement plainly — numbers and patterns, no hype in its own voice.

It reads facts/channels.md and facts/voice.md before every action, because platform tone is not interchangeable: what works as a Bluesky post is a bad Discord message.

A sample exchange

user   here's the post. where does it go?

agent  Bluesky now (your audience there is EU-evening), X as a two-post
       thread tomorrow 09:00, skip Discord — facts/channels.md says that
       server is support-only. Approve?

user   yes, and skip X

agent  Posted to Bluesky. I'll check replies in 4h and again tomorrow.

What it ships

FileWhat it is
PRODUCT.mdthe manifest — name, brand, tagline, env hints
SOUL.mdwho the agent is: proposes, waits, reports
facts/channels.mdthe accounts, their audiences, and what each is for
facts/voice.mdbrand voice and the do/don’t list

Who it is for

Anyone running more than one account who is losing time to the mechanics rather than the writing. Pair it with the content pipeline when drafting is also a bottleneck.
