Content pipeline (Inari)

Pitch, draft, revise, publish — in your voice, and never without your approval. The approval step is the product.

Inari drafts in your voice and publishes only after approval

Inari watches sources, proposes angles, drafts in the voice that facts/style.md records, revises on feedback and publishes only when you approve. It asks for a brief before it drafts, and it confirms the target platform before it finishes, because a Bluesky post and a newsletter section are different drafts.

Research drafts cite their sources. Final drafts remove the citations unless your style file asks for them. You need to check the claim, and your reader usually does not.

A sample exchange

user   anything worth writing about this week?

agent  Three angles. (1) the SQLite WAL post everyone is reacting to —
       contrarian take available. (2) your own migration story, no one
       has covered the rollback half. (3) a link roundup, cheap.
       Which, and for which platform?

user   2, bluesky thread

agent  Brief first: how technical, and do you want the outage named?

The product ships three files

FileWhat it is
PRODUCT.mdthe manifest: name, brand, tagline and env hints
SOUL.mdwho the agent is: drafts in your voice and waits for approval; runed renames it to PERSONA.md at the first turn
facts/style.mdthe voice, the tone, the topics in scope and the platform rules

The product also ships its tests in eval/.

A writer whose limit is drafting needs it

The product is for a person who publishes often and whose limit is drafting, not ideas. For distribution and scheduling on several platforms, use the social presence manager. The two work together: blend both into one group, and Inari drafts what Phosphene posts.
