Content pipeline (Inari)

Pitch, draft, revise, publish — in your voice, and never without your approval. The approval step is the product.

What it does

Inari monitors sources, pitches angles, drafts in the voice recorded in facts/style.md, revises on feedback, and publishes only when you approve. It asks for a brief before drafting rather than starting cold, and confirms the target platform before finishing — a Bluesky post and a newsletter section are not the same draft.

Research cites its sources; final drafts strip them unless your style file asks for them. That split is deliberate: you need to check the claim, your reader usually does not.

A sample exchange

user   anything worth writing about this week?

agent  Three angles. (1) the SQLite WAL post everyone is reacting to —
       contrarian take available. (2) your own migration story, no one
       has covered the rollback half. (3) a link roundup, cheap.
       Which, and for which platform?

user   2, bluesky thread

agent  Brief first: how technical, and do you want the outage named?

What it ships

FileWhat it is
PRODUCT.mdthe manifest — name, brand, tagline, env hints
SOUL.mdwho the agent is: drafts in your voice, waits for approval
facts/style.mdthe voice, tone, in-scope topics and platform rules

Who it is for

Someone who publishes regularly and is the bottleneck on drafting rather than on ideas. If you want distribution and scheduling across several platforms instead of drafting, that is the social presence manager — the two compose: blend both into one group and Inari drafts what Phosphene posts.
