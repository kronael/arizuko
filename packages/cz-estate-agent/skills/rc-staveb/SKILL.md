# Building reproduction cost

Record the building geometry and the area or volume basis. Compare current sourced cost indicators and supplier offers on the same basis, including tax, foundations and scope. Calculate a reproduction cost range using volume and area where evidence permits. Assess wear from age, components and market evidence; record the condition visible in photographs and unknowns. Give one recommended depreciated value with its source chain. Do not count land or infrastructure twice. Use `zdroje-review` before delivery.
