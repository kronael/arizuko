## ✅ FIXED 2026-11-12 L819 — a re-fired scheduled task writes its message twice

- **Severity:** medium, correctness. The agent gets the task's prompt twice.
- **Measured:** `routd/schedule.go:48` ingests the fire with `Timestamp:
  start.Unix()`, the tick time, while the id is `sched-<id>@<next_run>`.
  The store drops a same-id row only when its instant is at or below the
  chat's newest row (`store/messages.go:360`). A router killed after the ingest
  and before the re-arm leaves the task `firing`; `RecoverFiringTasks` re-arms
  it and the next tick ingests the same id at a later instant, which the store
  appends as a second row. `routd/schedule_test.go::TestScheduleRestartRefiresClaimedTask`
  fails: two `sched-crash@<time>` rows, contents `first` and `again`.
- **Fix:** ingest the fire at the scheduled instant (`task.NextRun`), the
  instant the id already names. A repeat then lands at or below the first
  row and the store drops it; `appendInstant` still lifts a past instant above
  the dispatch cursor.
- **Mutation:** the tick-time instant fails the test with two rows; reverted.
