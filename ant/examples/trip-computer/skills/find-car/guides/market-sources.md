# Market sources

Search several current marketplaces and dealers in the requested country. In Czech research, check Sauto, Tipcars and Bazoš. Record the listing link, date, price, year, mileage, seller and condition. Deduplicate listings and verify availability. Flag missing service history, uncertain import or accident history, and implausible prices; confirm seller ratings from their source.

Before recommending a listing from earlier research, ALWAYS open it again and record the new date, price and availability. NEVER quote an older observation as a current price.
