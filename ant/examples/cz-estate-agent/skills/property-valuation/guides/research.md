# Valuation research

Keep the detailed evidence in the group's research record. Identify the property from the land register and compare its parcel areas with the listing. Resolve differences before treating either as the sale boundary.

Prepare independent comparable-sales and component estimates. Record each comparable's date, source, location, size and condition. Distinguish completed sales from asking prices and group duplicate offers. Value land by parcel, use and zoning; check each zone against the plan and its printed parcel numbers. Use `rc-staveb` for building reproduction cost and wear. Reconcile the estimates and explain their difference. State missing evidence and its effect on the range. Do not count a premium twice.

Check an external appraisal as a document, not as proof of value. Identify its author, date, client, method and figures. Keep negotiation records separate from the valuation. Use `zdroje-review` to challenge the load-bearing sources and repair gaps or state their effect.
