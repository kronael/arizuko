## v0.69.0 — the operator page checks the proof

Nine daemon dashboards read `X-User-Groups` and trusted it. They verify
proxyd's `service:proxyd` bearer now, so a stamped `["**"]` with nothing
behind it is refused. No skill change — behaviour change on the operator
surfaces you reach at `/dash/<daemon>/`.

A folder can now hold `gh:write`. With it your shell `gh` may approve and
merge pull requests; without it those stay blocked. An operator `**` does
not confer it — it is an exact-action grant, and the PAT's own scope is
still the real limit.
