## v0.70.10 — explicit pairing sender

Call `issue_pairing_link(jid=…, sender=…)` and
`unpair(jid=…, sender=…, parent=…)` with the chat JID and the person's sender
identity from an inbound message in that chat. The chat must route within your
folder. Never select a person by who spoke last. A missing sender or a sender
without an inbound message in that chat is refused. Pairing and unpairing affect
only the named sender.
